2. What we sell

For a service fee we create, in the customer's name:

  • a genuine, unticketed airline reservation (a “hold” booking with a PNR code), delivered as an e-mail itinerary and PDF; and/or
  • a genuine, cancellable hotel reservation on a free-cancellation rate, delivered as an e-mail confirmation and PDF voucher.

The fee covers this service only. No flight ticket is purchased and the airline is never paid; a flight reservation cannot be used to board. A hotel reservation is held on a free-cancellation rate and cancelled before its deadline; it is not a paid stay.

3. Prices and payment

Prices are shown in euros and include VAT. You pay by card through Stripe or, where offered, in cryptocurrency through our payment provider. The contract is concluded when your payment is confirmed; we then create the reservation, usually within minutes.

4. Validity

A flight reservation remains valid until the airline's hold deadline (typically 24–72 hours). With the 7-day and 14-day plans we recreate the reservation with a new PNR each time it is released, until the end of the purchased period or the departure date, whichever comes first. Each renewal produces a new PNR and the old one becomes invalid; a renewal may use a different flight if the original is no longer available.

If you give us an appointment date, we create the flight reservation on the evening before that date (UTC) so that it is valid on the day, and e-mail it to you immediately.

A hotel reservation is made through our hotel booking partners on a rate with free cancellation and is cancelled by us at least 24 hours before the end of its free-cancellation period. It must not be relied on for an actual stay.
