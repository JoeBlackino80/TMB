How it works

  1. You pay the €{{ price }} service fee.
  2. We place a real reservation with the airline in your name — a booking with a PNR code, held without payment.
  3. The itinerary with the PNR arrives in your inbox, usually within minutes. You can verify it on the airline's own website (“Manage booking”).
  4. After 24–72 hours the unpaid booking is released by the airline automatically. Nothing to cancel, nothing more to pay.

What it is — and what it is not

You receive a real reservation, not an edited PDF: the PNR exists in the airline's system and anyone can verify it while it is valid.

It is not a flight ticket — you cannot board with it, and once it expires, verification will show it as released. Embassies commonly accept (and often recommend) unticketed reservations for visa applications; time your application so the reservation is live when it is checked, and order a fresh one whenever you need it again.
