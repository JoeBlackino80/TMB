FAQ

What people ask

Is it safe to enter my mailbox password?

The password is stored encrypted and used solely to read mail over an encrypted IMAP connection. We recommend an app password you can revoke at any time. And if you'd rather not enter a password at all, just forward invoices to your unique VORU address.

I don't know my mail server — is that a problem?

Not at all. When adding a mailbox you simply pick your provider (Gmail, Outlook and others) and the technical details are filled in automatically. The connection guide shows it step by step — and if you get stuck, write to us and we'll set it up with you.

Which banks do the QR payments work with?

All Slovak banks (PAY by square), Czech banks (QR Platba), Hungarian banks (MNB QR) and EPC/SEPA QR codes accepted by banking apps across the euro area, including Austria and Germany.

How do payments get marked as paid?

The most convenient way: forward your bank statement and VORU matches the payments itself. Or click the button right in the email, reply "paid 3", or tick it off in the app — whatever suits you.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. Registration is card-free and everything is free for 14 days. If you decide to continue, you pay by card via Stripe.

I run several companies — can VORU handle that?

Yes. Add any number of mailboxes for different companies to one account and everything comes together in one overview and one email.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes — install VORU on your phone's home screen straight from the browser, no App Store needed. iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → menu → Install app. Everything important also arrives by email, so you barely need to open the app.
